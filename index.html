02

Core skills clients are looking for

Automation & workflow building 27/30 required

Multi-branch GHL workflows with triggers, conditional logic, wait steps — not just basic setups.

CRM management 26/30 required

Pipelines, tags, contact hygiene, lead routing, stages, and opportunity tracking — heavily GHL-focused.

Email & SMS automation 22/30 required

Follow-up sequences, appointment reminders, missed call text-back, voicemail drops, and drip campaigns.

SOP documentation 20/30 high demand

Document every system clearly enough for someone else to maintain. Loom walkthroughs are increasingly expected.

Funnel building 18/30 required

Sales pages, opt-ins, VSLs, upsells, thank-you pages inside GHL, Systeme.io, or Kajabi.

AI tool usage 18/30 high demand

Use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini to build AI agents, speed up SOPs, QA automations, and improve workflows.

API & webhook integrations 16/30 required

REST APIs, JSON payloads, OAuth, webhooks to connect platforms. This separates basic VAs from systems specialists.

Reporting & dashboards 14/30 high demand

Google Sheets with lookups, pivots, and live data from GHL or ad platforms.

03

New vs. previous batch — what changed

n8n jumped significantly

From 3/15 jobs to 11/30 — it's no longer a fringe tool.

AI agents are a new category

New postings explicitly ask for AI agent building — chatbots, lead qualification bots, and AI-powered workflow automation.

Web dev skills gaining traction

Some jobs now ask for WordPress, HTML/CSS, and even Node.js/PHP.

Tips for Days 1–30
  • Sign up for a GoHighLevel free trial — use a dummy agency name
  • Watch one YouTube tutorial per topic before doing it yourself — then close the video and do it from memory
  • Don't aim for perfection. Ugly funnels that work beat beautiful funnels that don't exist
  • Screenshot every step you build — these become your portfolio evidence
